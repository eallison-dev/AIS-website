What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to work more efficiently, and to provide information to the website owner. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way.

Categories of Cookies We May Introduce

If AI Springboard introduces cookies in future, they will fall into one or more of the categories below. We will update this notice, and add a consent banner where required before any non-essential cookies are set.

Strictly necessary cookies

Required for the website to function, for example to remember security settings. These do not require consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Analytics and performance cookies

Would help us understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are most visited, so we can improve it. These would require your consent before being set.

Functional cookies

Would remember choices you make on the site, such as preferences, to provide a more personalised experience. These would require your consent unless strictly necessary to a service you have requested.

Marketing and advertising cookies

Would be used to track visitors across websites for advertising purposes. AI Springboard has no current plans to use cookies of this kind. These would always require your consent.

Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. General guidance for managing cookies in most major browsers is available at www.aboutcookies.org, or through your browser’s own help pages. Blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites, though not this one, given its current build.
